QUICK UPDATE GUIDE

1. Add Your Photo
   - Put your photo as "beverly.jpg" in this folder
   - If not added, it shows a placeholder

2. Contact Form
   - Using FormSubmit.co - messages go directly to prinsnics@gmail.com
   - On first submission, FormSubmit will send you a confirmation email - click it to activate
   - After activation, all messages will go straight to your Gmail

3. Customize
   - Update LinkedIn/Instagram links in index.html (search for href="#")
   - Projects section can be updated with your actual work
   - Colors in style.css under :root variables

4. View Locally
   - Open index.html in browser, or run: python -m http.server 8000 then go to localhost:8000
   - Or just double-click index.html

Done! Everything is set up with your info and email.
